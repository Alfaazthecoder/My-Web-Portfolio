✦ About Me

Hi, I'm Alfaaz.

Photographer & Visual Storyteller

I'm a self-taught photographer based in Abu Dhabi, obsessed with exploring the subtle spaces where concrete structures meet wild biological life. Looking through my lens, I search for instances where technology and nature find a symbiotic balance rather than fighting for dominance.

This blog is built to showcase my growth, experiments, and ongoing thoughts as an amateur creator studying the environment and modern digital infrastructure.
