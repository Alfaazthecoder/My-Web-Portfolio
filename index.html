✦ About Me

Hi, I'm Alfaaz.

Photographer & Visual Storyteller

I'm a self-taught photographer based in Abu Dhabi, obsessed with capturing the intersection of desert light, golden coastlines, and the fleeting moments that make the Gulf unique. Every frame is a story waiting to be told.

When I'm not behind the lens, I'm writing about the craft — the gear, the process, the philosophy of slowing down to truly see the world around us.
